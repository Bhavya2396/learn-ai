Sound Needs a Medium

Lock an electric bell inside a glass jar, switch it on, then suck out all the air. The bell keeps ringing — you can SEE the hammer striking — but the sound fades and finally vanishes. Pump the air back in and the sound returns. This is the cleanest, most famous proof in all of acoustics: sound needs matter to travel through. No medium, no sound.
